# Film grain design

- The shared grain parameter object selects one engine and carries its controls.
- Fields for inactive engines are ignored.
- Dye's algorithm contract is defined in its [design](dye/design.md).
- Fotufilm Film's controls are defined in its [product contract](fotufilm/spec.md#parameters).
- Profile resolution happens before rendering: named Procedural profiles provide defaults, and `custom` applies explicit overrides. Validation rejects unknown profiles, non-finite values, and out-of-range controls.
- Spektra owns the density-domain sampler.
- Procedural owns the encoded-RGB procedural model.
- Exposure owns the reconstructed post-scan encoded-RGB value-noise appearance model.
- Neither engine calls the other or shares an approximation layer.
- Fotufilm Film owns the fitted-population residual that core applies at its own declared film-development boundary.
- Exposure shares the post-scan boundary, not the Procedural algorithm or controls.
- Backend selection and device residency follow [GPU execution](../gpu-execution/spec.md#backend-selection) and its [device-residency design](../gpu-execution/design.md#device-residency).
- Each engine MUST expose its declared model to both the CPU reference executor and GPU production executor. No engine-specific CPU-only route or approximation is allowed.
- The caller receives one grain result in the engine's declared domain. Color conversion and transfer encoding are not duplicated around the effect.
- Grain MUST remain a stage of the film chain, not a separate public pipeline or generic effect graph.
- Dye owns the conditional film-coordinate dye field and area readout. Its [design](dye/design.md) defines parameter resolution, full-film geometry, count statistics, executor alignment, and numerical certification.
- Its [product contract](dye/spec.md) defines supported conditions and photographic acceptance.
- Fotufilm Film supplies a model that core resolves against the loaded film curve. Its [design](fotufilm/design.md) defines density adaptation, formation and calibration, complete periodic footprints, geometry, precision, and evidence; its [product contract](fotufilm/spec.md) defines parameters, dispatch, and acceptance.
- Exposure owns a reconstructed post-scan encoded-RGB appearance. Its [design](exposure/design.md) defines the frozen scalar reference, table and executor boundary, and declared Color Variation limitation; its [product contract](exposure/spec.md) defines controls and acceptance.
