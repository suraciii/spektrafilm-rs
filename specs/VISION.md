# Product Vision

SpektraFilm exists to simulate analog color film and the print-and-scan chain spectrally, through measured spectral data rather than color presets. It serves photographers, filmmakers, and colorists who want to apply film looks to digital images with physical accuracy.

A separate LUT workflow provides non-spectral color looks for workflows that do not need spectral material simulation. It does not replace the spectral film chain or claim physical film accuracy.

Spectral film looks can be exported as complete LUT files. A LUT file is imported explicitly when the LUT workflow is wanted; export never applies it or creates a preset.

The preset library carries both film look presets and LUT presets; applying either selects the workflow it describes. A LUT workflow result is a color transform, not a spectral claim.
