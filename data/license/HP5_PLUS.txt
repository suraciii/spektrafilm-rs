ILFORD HP5 PLUS — source notice

The profile and built-in look reconstruct coordinate data from:
ILFORD HP5 PLUS Technical Information, November 2018 (6 pages),
page 5 characteristic curve and page 1 wedge spectrogram.

The spectral ordinate is printed only as "Sensitivity" with 0.5/1.0 ticks.
This implementation treats it as linear normalized sensitivity with zero at
the frame bottom; that is an explicit interpretation, not a statement printed
by Harman. Both linear and log readings can be anchored to the same ticks.
The p1 curve is a 2850 K tungsten wedge response with no equal-energy
correction; it is not equal-energy film sensitivity.

The 380-355 nm and 660-780 nm tails in the profile are endpoint-slope
extrapolations clamped at 1e-12, not measured. `densitometer=diffuse_visual`
and `antihalation=weak` are conservative metadata assumptions.

No physical film, scanner, grain, halation or color-accuracy calibration is
claimed. This is an internal research profile, not a manufacturer-endorsed
reproduction.
