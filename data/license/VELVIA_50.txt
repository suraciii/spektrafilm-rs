Calibration uses the bundled Hanatos2025 spectral LUT and its existing license
and attribution, and CIE 2017 TCS/BabelColor data distributed by Colour Science.
Calibration data are consumed from the pinned colour-science package and are
not copied into this source directory.

Changes, 2026-10-11: reconstructed CMY amounts by forward Status A integration;
inferred a smooth clear-film base; fitted three decreasing Gaussian-CDF
components; normalized the direct-positive scan reference; independently fitted
Hanatos window and surface parameters. The upstream borrowed grain curve and
color_masking adjustment were not imported.

Source URLs, hashes, assumptions and reproduction steps are recorded in
../profile_sources/fujifilm_velvia_50.json and ../../docs/profile-preparation.md.
