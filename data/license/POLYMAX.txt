Characteristic and threshold-sensitivity coordinates were extracted directly
from the publication's vector centerlines. Jan Lohse's independent digitization
was used as a cross-check only; its point dictionaries are not copied into the
shipped source arrays. The pinned cross-check source is:
https://github.com/JanLohse/spectral_film_lut/blob/6307a98f3cf4f2d7b6d09bab82fbf6f64356ccfe/src/spectral_film_lut/bw_print_film/kodak_polymax_fine_art.py

The profile uses neutral silver and base approximations, one effective threshold
sensitivity, explicit unmeasured spectral tails, and the unfiltered condition.
It does not identify physical emulsion components, fluorescence, paper texture,
BRDF, or arbitrary-filter variable contrast. No other stock supplies its curve.
