Numerical RGB adaptation uses existing SpektraFilm/Colour Science calibration
code, CIE 2017 test reflectances and BabelColor Average validation patches.
It is not measured Tri-X photograph calibration. Neutral silver/base, spectral
tails and an aggregate density record are explicit model assumptions.
