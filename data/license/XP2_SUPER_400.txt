ILFORD XP2 SUPER 400 — source notice

The profile and built-in look reconstruct coordinate data from:
ILFORD XP2 SUPER Technical Information, November 2018 (5 pages),
page 2 wedge spectrogram and page 3 characteristic curve.

The technical sheet is published by Harman technology Limited / ILFORD.

The spectral ordinate is printed only as "Sensitivity" with 0.5/1.0 ticks.
This implementation treats it as linear normalized sensitivity with zero at
the frame bottom; that is an explicit interpretation, not a statement printed
by Harman. The curve is a 2856 K tungsten wedge response with no equal-energy
correction; it is not equal-energy film sensitivity.

The 380-355 nm and 660-780 nm tails in the profile are endpoint-slope
extrapolations clamped at 1e-12, not measured.

XP2 SUPER is a chromogenic B&W film: its image is made of dyes, not silver.
The datasheet publishes no RMS granularity or MTF, so the built-in look uses
the Dye grain engine with an explicitly class-level, uncalibrated strength.
`densitometer=diffuse_visual` and `antihalation=weak` are conservative metadata
assumptions. No physical film, scanner, grain, halation or color-accuracy
calibration is claimed. This is an internal research profile, not a
manufacturer-endorsed reproduction.
