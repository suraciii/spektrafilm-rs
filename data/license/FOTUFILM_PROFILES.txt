Fotufilm-derived film profiles
==============================

The following profiles in data/profiles/ are adapted from Fotufilm film
profiles by MUAStudio Inc. (Fotufilm, https://fotufilm.com), source
https://github.com/DhaliwalX/fotufilm-engine at commit
96623ba0831a52dbddcda35727a206d4123a8b5f. They were converted to the
spektrafilm/Negs profile format by the Negs project at
https://github.com/noahhaasis/negs commit
26c903be9064b4fbfd46a8396620ba7e1e9bc1df, and are adapted as noted below:

  ilford_delta_3200
  ilford_fp4_plus

The conversions rebalance sensitivity to Negs' neutral-exposure convention,
resample and fit the characteristic curves, and (for the two B&W profiles)
gather development conditions into a time family. Each profile's metadata
records these changes.
