Kodak Vericolor II Type L (4108) public-data reconstruction
============================================================

Source publication
------------------
Kodak Publication E-77, "Kodak Color Films", September 1980,
data sheet DS-8, page 62: KODAK VERICOLOR II Professional Film 4108,
Type L.
Public scan:
http://photomemorabilia.co.uk/Colour_Darkroom/MFisher/Kodak_%20Color_Films_E77_DataBook_Sep1980.pdf
SHA-256 of the copy used:
5e308ce1114ca57a7a644dc047982609e315244341efb658aeed7b69e4fc3cb5

New contributions
------------------
The profile digitizes and normalizes the published characteristic curves,
spectral sensitivity, D-min, and mid-scale combined spectral density. It fits
the Status M characteristic curves with the donor dye basis. These new
contributions, the profile JSON, and the built-in preset are licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Donor and proxy data
--------------------
DS-8 does not publish separate C/M/Y dye spectra. The channel_density vectors
are inherited from the kodak_portra_160 profile by Andrea Volpato, licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. The print target uses the bundled Kodak Ektacolor Edge profile.
E-19 and E-7020 publication data describe that print proxy; they are not
measurements of Vericolor II film.

Limits
------
No film or paper capture was made. The profile is a public-data
approximation and does not establish stock, paper, scanner, or aging
fidelity. Do not describe it as a measured reproduction.
