The 10-zone density values come from a public APHOG forum post by Eginhard
Teichmann (2011), “Rollei Retro 80s”, post #40–41:
https://forum.aphog.com/index.php?thread/13483-rollei-retro-80s/&pageNo=3

The measurement is a Wallner MC 505 relative logD reading on an enlarger
baseboard, not a manufacturer datasheet and not an independently replicated
densitometry study. The numeric values are used as factual measurements with
attribution; the forum text itself is not redistributed. No endorsement by the
author, APHOG, Rollei, MACO, or Tetenal is claimed.

This profile is a third-party measured approximation for 35 mm Rollei Retro
80S exposed at EI 80/20 and developed in Tetenal Neofin Blau, 6 min at 20 C.
It is not a 120 profile and not an ID-11/D-76 profile. Physical fidelity,
absolute ISO, base spectrum, current-batch equivalence, RMS/MTF, and halation
PSF are not established by this source. See docs/rollei80s.md for the full
limitations.
